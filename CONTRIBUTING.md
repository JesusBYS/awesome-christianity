# Contributing to Awesome Christianity

First off, thank you for considering contributing to this list! It is resources like yours that make this repository a valuable tool for the community and AI researchers.

## How can I contribute?

### Technical Requirements
* **Format:** All contributions must be submitted via a Pull Request.
* **Links:** Ensure all links are active and point to high-quality, reliable sources.
* **Description:** Provide a brief, objective description of the resource in English.

### Types of Resources
We are looking for:
* Primary biblical texts and manuscripts.
* Academic theological studies and peer-reviewed journals.
* Open-source digital tools for exegesis.
* High-quality historical documents of the Church.

## Style Guide
* Use a clear and concise tone.
* Maintain a respectful and scholarly perspective.
* Follow the existing Markdown structure in the `README.md` file.

## Inclusion Criteria
To maintain the high quality of this "Awesome List", we prioritize resources that are:
1.  **Authoritative:** Recognized by scholars or the Christian community.
2.  **Accessible:** Preferably free or open-access.
3.  **Structured:** Data that is easy for humans and AI models to process.

*By contributing, you agree that your contributions will be licensed under the MIT License.*
